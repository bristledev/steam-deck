# Beyond Steam

The Steam Deck is a PC, so Steam isn't the only place your games can come from. This chapter covers the two most popular community tools for other stores and older consoles, and how to put anything you install into your Steam library.

## Epic, GOG and Amazon: Heroic Games Launcher

The official launchers from other stores are made for Windows, and many don't run well on Linux. The community-built **[Heroic Games Launcher](https://heroicgameslauncher.com/)** handles three of them natively: **[Epic Games](https://www.epicgames.com/)**, **[GOG](https://www.gog.com/)** and **Amazon Games**.

1. Switch to **Desktop Mode**.
2. Open **Discover**, search for **Heroic** and click **Install**.
3. Open Heroic and log in to your accounts.

From there, you can download your games and run them with Proton, just like Steam does. To play them from Game Mode, use Heroic's **Add to Steam** option on a game, or switch on **Add games to Steam automatically** in its settings.

## Emulators: EmuDeck

**[EmuDeck](https://www.emudeck.com/)** sets up emulators for older consoles, like Nintendo and PlayStation systems, with settings tuned for the Deck. It also creates the folders for your games and can add them to your Steam library.

1. In Desktop Mode, download the EmuDeck installer from **[EmuDeck.com](https://www.emudeck.com/)**.
2. Move the installer to your desktop and run it.
3. Follow its setup steps. When it's done, put your games in the `Emulation/roms` folder it creates.

The **[EmuDeck wiki](https://emudeck.github.io/how-to-install-emudeck/steamos/)** walks through the full setup, including adding your games to Steam with Steam ROM Manager.

> [!NOTE]
> **About ROMs:** emulators themselves are legal, but downloading game files (ROMs) from the internet generally isn't, even for games you own. Make backups from your own cartridges and discs instead.

## Adding Any App to Steam

Steam can launch more than Steam games. Adding a program to your library lets you open it from Game Mode:

1. Open Steam in Desktop Mode.
2. Go to **Games → Add a Non-Steam Game to My Library...**
3. Tick the apps you want, like Discord or Firefox, and click **Add Selected Programs**.

The app now appears in your library alongside your games.

Between Discover, Heroic and EmuDeck, you can install most things. A few apps aren't in any store at all, though, and Linux has its own version of the "download and double-click" app for those.
